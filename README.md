## Business context

Short-term solar irradiance forecasting plays a central role in energy system operations. Utilities, grid operators, and solar developers depend on accurate predictions of solar power availability to balance supply and demand, schedule reserves, and minimize curtailment. One of the most useful quantities in this context is Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI) --- a measure of the total solar energy received per unit area on a horizontal surface.
